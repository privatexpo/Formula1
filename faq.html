Tickets FAQ

Buying, grandstands, payment, delivery and how to spot a ticket scam.

Understand Formula 1

A short guide to the cars, the sessions and how a race weekend works.

How can I purchase tickets?

Open a Grand Prix from the calendar or the Tickets page, choose a category, and add it to the Basket. Place order opens the secure payment page. The order is confirmed when the payment succeeds.

You can also buy through the race promoter or an appointed agent for that event. Buy only on a secure checkout page.

Where can I purchase genuine F1 tickets and how can I avoid ticket scams?

Buy race tickets and hospitality, including Paddock Club™, on this store, from the race promoter, or from an appointed agent.

Red flags to watch for:

  • Persistent or unsolicited sales emails from a site you do not remember registering with
  • Heavily discounted race or Paddock Club™ tickets
  • Pressure about “last remaining tickets”
  • Grammatical errors on websites, emails, or social posts
  • Odd email addresses, or consumer addresses such as @outlook or @gmail instead of a business domain
  • Payment requests over WhatsApp or social messages to a personal bank account instead of a secure portal
  • Any claimed FIA endorsement or confirmatory letter — the FIA regulates the sport and does not sell tickets

Formula 1 cannot be held responsible for dealings with unofficial vendors. Stay vigilant.

How do I log in to my personal ticketing account?

Your order is tied to the email you enter at checkout. The Basket icon at the top right keeps the tickets you have selected on this device until you place the order or remove them.

The sign-in on Formula1.com is for F1 Access. It is not the account for this ticket store.

Where can I find the race timetable?

Session times are published on formula1.com/en/racing. Open the Grand Prix and the full timetable. Start times can move closer to the weekend, so check again before you travel.

When are the event dates confirmed?

The FIA confirms the season calendar, usually around December. Dates on this store are the 2027 race weekends. If a date is still marked TBC, you are buying the event, not a locked calendar day.

How do I know whether the tickets I want are still available?

A category with Select is on sale. Add it to the Basket while it is listed. If a category is sold out, join the waitlist and we will email you if tickets are released.

How can I find the characteristics of a grandstand?

On the Tickets page, each category shows the grandstand name, Friday to Sunday, and the weekend price. Select the row to highlight that stand on the circuit map.

Can I request a special seat or adjoining seats?

You cannot pick an exact seat number online. Tickets in the same order are seated together where possible, but that is not guaranteed. Three-day and single-day tickets in the same grandstand are not usually seated together. Final allocation is made closer to the event. After you order, use Contact us if you need a seating note.

Are tickets for children or parking available?

Prices shown are adult weekend rates. Child tickets, age rules and parking depend on the Grand Prix and appear on the category when they are offered. A child ticket is not a place in an adult grandstand unless the description says so.

How do I know if a grandstand is wheelchair accessible?

Wheelchair spaces and companion seats depend on the circuit. Contact us before you travel with the Grand Prix, the category and the access you need, and we will confirm what that grandstand can offer.

Can I cancel, modify, or add to an order?

You can change the quantity or remove a line in the Basket before you place the order. Once the order is confirmed, tickets are not cancellable or changeable. To add seats, place a new order while stock remains. Seating notes go through Contact us.

Can I get a refund if I cannot attend, or if the event is postponed?

Tickets are non-refundable if you cannot attend. If the organiser postpones, reschedules or cancels the Grand Prix, we email the address on the order with the options for that event.

What is the Basket, and how do I use it?

The Basket icon at the top right holds your tickets before checkout. Use + and − to change the quantity, and remove any line you do not want. The basket stays on this device until you check out or clear it.

How many tickets can I purchase at once?

You can buy up to 8 tickets per category in one order, while stock lasts. For a larger group, contact us with the Grand Prix and the category.

What payment methods are accepted?

Place the order with the name and email on the tickets. Payment plans are not offered. Bank transfer can be arranged for hospitality through Contact us.

Never send card details by email, SMS, WhatsApp or social media.

Why might the amount or currency look different?

Every price on this store is in euros. The amount in the Basket is the amount of the order. If a handling fee applies to an event, it is shown before you confirm.

Can I use the Official F1 Timing App to get my tickets?

No. The Official F1® Timing App is for event information and live timing. It does not issue or store tickets. Your tickets are in the confirmation sent to the email on the order.

What are e-tickets and mobile tickets? How will I obtain real tickets?

Delivery depends on the Grand Prix and the category: courier, downloadable e-ticket, mobile ticket, or collection at the circuit. E-tickets open in your account 10 days before the weekend and stay valid through race day. A physical ticket cannot be swapped for an e-ticket after purchase, or the reverse, unless that event allows it.

Can I transfer or print my tickets?

An e-ticket can be printed after you download it. A mobile ticket stays on the phone and usually cannot be printed. You can transfer a ticket to someone else when the event allows it. Once it is transferred, we may not be able to reissue it.

How does delivery work?

Posted tickets usually leave 10 days to 3 weeks before the event, with a tracking number. Someone needs to sign during the day. Short-stay rental addresses are often refused. The delivery cost depends on the destination country and is shown at checkout. A hotel can take the parcel only if the hotel accepts courier deliveries. Collection needs the voucher and a photo ID.

If the tickets have not arrived before you travel, contact us as early as you can.
