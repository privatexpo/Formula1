Your order

Retrieve booking

Look up a paid or pending order with the email used at payment and the order number on the confirmation.

The number starts with F1- and is on the payment confirmation. It is not the name of the Grand Prix.

What you need

  1. The email entered on the payment page.
  2. The order number from that same confirmation.

What you will see

  • Whether the order is awaiting payment, paid, or cancelled.
  • One e-ticket per person, with a QR once the payment is confirmed.
  • The same passes the next time you open My tickets on this device.

Both fields have to match the same order. A wrong email returns no booking, even if the number is right.
