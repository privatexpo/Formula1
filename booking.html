Your order

Retrieve booking

Open your tickets with the email from the payment and the password sent after payment.

The password is in the confirmation email. It is not the order number.

What you need

  1. The email entered on the payment page.
  2. The password in the confirmation email.

What you will see

  • Whether the order is awaiting payment, paid, or cancelled.
  • One e-ticket per person. The QR opens 10 days before the weekend.
  • The same passes the next time you open My tickets on this device.

Both fields have to match the same order. A wrong password returns no booking, even if the email is right.
