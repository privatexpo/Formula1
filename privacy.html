Privacy policy

What we collect when you browse, write to us, join a waitlist, or pay for a ticket.

Last updated 30 September 2026.

1. Who is responsible

FEVER operates this store and is responsible for the personal data described here. Formula One Licensing BV is not the recipient of your order details.

2. What we collect

  • Basket. The Grand Prix, the category and the quantity stay in your browser until you empty the basket, pay, or clear the site data. We do not receive that basket until you place the order.
  • Order. When you pay, the payment page collects the email and the card details needed to take the payment. We receive the result of the payment, the amount, the tickets, and the email used for the confirmation. We do not receive or store the card number.
  • Contact form. Name, email and the message you type.
  • Waitlist. Name and email, plus the race you asked about. That list stays in your browser.
  • Technical data. The host that serves the site can log the usual connection data, such as IP address and the page requested, to keep the site running and to block abuse.

3. Why

  • To take the order, send the confirmation, and deliver the tickets.
  • To answer a message sent with Contact us.
  • To tell you if a sold-out race you joined the waitlist for goes on sale again. The waitlist itself is kept on your device until you send it.
  • To prevent fraud and to keep a record of the payment, which we need for accounting and for a possible refund.

The legal bases are the contract for the order, our legitimate interest in answering you and protecting the store, and a legal duty where we must keep a payment record.

4. Payment

Payment is handled on a hosted page for this store. The card number, the expiry date and the security code are typed there. They are not written into this website and they are not placed in an order note. We keep the payment reference and the paid or unpaid status so we can match the order.

5. Who else sees it

  • The payment provider, to charge the card and to tell us whether the payment succeeded.
  • The shop system that records the order, so the tickets can be issued and a refund can be made.
  • The email service that sends the confirmation, when that service is switched on.

We do not sell the list of buyers. We do not use your email for a newsletter. We do not pass the order to Formula One Licensing BV.

6. How long

An order and its payment record are kept for the time we need to deliver the tickets and for the accounting period that applies to that sale. A contact message is kept long enough to reply and to deal with a follow-up. The basket and the waitlist in your browser stay until you remove them, or until the browser clears site data.

7. Cookies

This store does not set an advertising cookie and it does not run a marketing pixel. The basket and the waitlist are stored in the browser’s local storage, not in a tracking cookie. That storage is required for the basket to survive a refresh. You can clear it in your browser settings. Clearing it empties the basket on this device.

The payment page is a separate site. It can set the cookies it needs to take the payment. Those cookies are described by that page, and they are not read back by this store.

8. Your choices

You can ask for a copy of the order data we hold, ask us to correct an email, or ask us to delete a contact message. A paid order is kept when the law requires a record of the sale. To ask, use and say which email was used at payment.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. In France, that authority is the CNIL.
